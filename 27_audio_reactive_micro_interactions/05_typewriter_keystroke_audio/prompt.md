# AI Prompt: Mechanical Keystroke Audio & Typewriter Interaction

```markdown
Create a realistic mechanical keyboard typewriter experience with procedural Web Audio synthesis for individual keystrokes, spacebars, and carriage return bell rings.

Key Requirements:
1. Procedural Switch Audio Synthesis:
   - Regular Keys (Cherry MX Blue): Dual-tone click with randomized high transient click (1800-2400Hz sawtooth sweep) plus low bottom-out thud (300Hz sine drop).
   - Spacebar: Low-frequency 220Hz triangle drop with rounded wooden timbre.
   - Enter / Carriage Return: High 1760Hz (A6) pure sine chime bell with 800ms exponential tail decay.
2. Typewriter Timing & Animation:
   - Natural human typing rhythm jitter (randomized 55ms-120ms intervals).
   - Monospace terminal window styling with blinking cursor.
   - Support both auto-typing simulation and live physical keyboard typing.
```
